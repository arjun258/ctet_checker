About This CTET Answer Checker

This free tool lets you check your CTET Paper 2 answers instantly using the official answer key released on the official CTET website. Simply upload a photo of your filled OMR sheet, select your subject, sets, and languages, and get your complete score breakdown in seconds.

Supports all question sets — Set G, Set H, Set I, and Set J — for both subject sections (Maths & Science and Social Science) and language sections (Hindi and English). The checker covers all 150 questions: CDP section (Q1-30), subject-specific questions (Q31-90), Language 1 (Q91-120), and Language 2 (Q121-150).

There is no negative marking. Questions marked Z in the official key are automatically awarded full marks.
